# Worker-prohibited maintenance or rollback

Parent only: load before considering worker-prohibited maintenance or rollback. Worker prohibitions remain unchanged.

Only the parent may act, with explicit human authorization for the operation itself, established exact scope, ownership and preservation, and available permitted tools. Fresh runtime/native consent and guards remain binding. Broad feature authorization is not destructive authority. Never bypass a denial via another worker or tool.

Upgrade: require a validated backup, artifact and migration validation where applicable, and a recovery path.

Rollback: preserve unrelated edits even in the same file, remove only proven task-created helpers, and never blanket restore, reset or clean.

Do not proceed with ambiguous ownership, a denial or any missing prerequisite. This route is guidance, not authorization.
