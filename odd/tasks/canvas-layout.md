# Give the design canvas a free layout and a device selector

Move artboards anywhere on the canvas, arrange them as a row, a column or a grid, show minimap titles at a glance, and make an open artboard use its space, with a device selector in the top bar.
Branch feat/canvas-layout (from feat/canvas-trim).

## Specs

- S1: "necesito poder mover las propuestas a mi antojo y en cualquier parte del canvas" (L1). Dragging an artboard's title (its label header) moves that artboard anywhere on the canvas, at any zoom ("Arrastrando su título", L2). Dragging the artboard body or the empty canvas still pans, a still click on the artboard still opens it, and buttons and links in the header keep working.
- S2: "deberia poder permitir ponerlo en vertical u horizontal a mi antojo" (L1); "Fila, columna y cuadrícula" (L2). A layout control arranges every artboard as a row (horizontal), a column (vertical) or a grid; after an arrangement each artboard can still be moved by hand (S1).
- S3: "En un archivo del diseño" (L2). Positions and the chosen arrangement are saved in `design/<slug>/layout.json` through the canvas server, validated before writing, and restored when the design is opened again in any browser. A design without `layout.json` opens in the row layout it has today. Artboards added later get a free spot; artboards removed from the design drop out of the layout. Fit, the minimap, focus mode and the help card keep working with moved artboards.
- S4 (withdrawn, L20): "en el minimapa si me pongo encima de una propuesta aparece el titulo de la propuesta pero esto deberia poder verse a primera vista si es posible" (L1). The minimap shows each artboard's title (or at least its number and as much title as fits) without hovering.
- S5: "cuando se abre una propuesta se pierde mucho espacio porque hay muchos margenes" (L1). An open (focused) artboard uses noticeably more of the viewport: smaller margins and a compact header.
- S6: "al igual que hemos hecho con el selector de idiomas me gustaria añadir un selector de dispositivos que permita seleccionar movil, tablet escritorio o custom y eliminar los dispositivos de su ubicacion actual" (L1); "Solo con una propuesta abierta" (L2). A device selector in the top bar, built like the language selector, offers Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Custom (a width) and All sizes. It acts only while an artboard is open. The per-artboard size bar in the artboard header goes away.
- S8: "sigo viendo mucho margen por los lados al abrir una propuesta" (L6); "Ancho de la ventana" and "Sí, ocultarlas" (L7). In focus mode, Desktop shows the artboard at 100% with the full available viewport width (Mobile, Tablet and Custom keep their fixed width), and the other artboards are hidden until focus ends.
- S9: "lo de mover la propuesta solo con el titulo funciona pero el area para moverla es muy pequeña" (L8); "Arrastrando cualquier parte (Recomendado)" (L9). Outside focus mode, dragging any part of an artboard (its title or its content) moves it and a still click still opens it; the canvas pans by dragging the empty background, with the wheel or trackpad, with the minimap, or by holding Space while dragging anywhere. This supersedes S1's "Dragging the artboard body ... still pans".
- S10: "y al hacer el click para moverlo que se seleccione todo el area de la propuesta para ver que esta seleccionada" (L10). Pressing an artboard to move it outlines the whole artboard (title and frame) as selected; after the drop it stays selected until a press elsewhere or Esc.
- S11: "y tambien los shortcus hay que permitir que el usuario pueda configurar los shortcus como quiera y que no esten fijos" (L11); "En este navegador" and "Los de herramientas y paneles" (L12). Every single-key tool and panel shortcut (Move V, Comment C, Overview F, Minimap M, review panel ], help ?, and any other single-key action) can be reassigned from the shortcuts list to another single key; a key already used by another action is refused with a message naming that action; each action can be reset and all can be restored to the defaults. Custom keys are saved in this browser (localStorage, like the language) for every design, and every place that shows a key (rail tooltips, kbd hints, the minimap title, the help) shows the current one. Esc, Shift+Esc, arrows, Space and Enter stay fixed.
- S13: "lo que si, esto en el header sobra" (L16, screenshot of the top-bar chosen status: "«Brand rail» · a las 28 oct, 15:46 · Ver"). The chosen artboard's status in the top bar (title, state and since when, the agent's note, Show) goes away: markup, styles, strings only it uses, tests and docs. The state stays in the chosen artboard's header and the review panel's Handoff card; Compare with app stays in the top bar. Step-by-step help steps that pointed at it point at the chosen artboard instead.
- S14: "y el check del conectado hazlo mas sutil y ponlo a la derecha del todo" (L17, screenshot of the top bar: "● Conectado" between the zoom group and Compare with app). The connection indicator moves to the far right end of the top bar (after Comments) and is subtler: while connected it is only a small dot, its state in the tooltip and accessible name; while reconnecting or disconnected it shows its short text too, so a lost connection is still noticed.
- S15: "la configuracion de atajos del teclado esta perfecta pero vamos a sustituir atajos de teclado por configure(icono de engranaje) y dentro separados por tabs irán los shortcuts configurables, el theme que ademas de tener el theme gentle quiero añadir un theme neutro/gris y el selector del idioma, la activacion de la ayuda y el sistema de diseño, por lo que la opcion ... mas pasaria a ser el icono del engranaje con todo lo comentado" (L21), superseded by L23-L25: "para, lo quiero mejor asi" (screenshot of the More menu with a new "apariencia" entry above Sistema de diseño, Atajos de teclado, Ayuda paso a paso); "un popup igual que el de atajos de teclado"; "También meter el idioma en Apariencia"; "pero el mas si se cambia al engranaje y cada opcion". Result: the rail's More (…) button becomes a Settings button with a gear icon that opens the same menu as today with a new first entry, Appearance, followed by Design system, Keyboard shortcuts and Step-by-step help, each doing what it does today. Appearance opens a dialog like the Keyboard shortcuts one with the theme (Gentle, the current pink one, and Neutral, a dark gray theme with neutral grays and a light-gray accent instead of the pink; saved in this browser and applied on load) and the language (moved there from the top bar, with its auto option and note). No tabs.
- S16: "arregla la maquetacuion cuando se va a añadir un comentario, no se ven los botones alineados" (L27, screenshot of the comment popover with Adjuntar captura, Cancelar / Implementar solo esto and Enviar on three rows). The popover's actions sit on one row: Implement only this on the left, Cancel and Send together on the right.
- S17: "añade la posibilidad de editar el comentario antes de enviarlo al agente" (L27); "Editar también los enviados" (L28). Every open comment, sent or not, has an Edit action on its card in the review panel; it edits the text in place (Save / Cancel), validated like a new comment (1 to 2000 characters) and saved to `comments.json` through the canvas server. Saving a changed text of a comment already sent also sends the new text to the session as an edit of that comment (naming the previous text); an unsent one just waits for Send all. A resolved comment cannot be edited (the server refuses it). The screenshot is not changed by an edit.
- S18: "aqui en apariencia gentle cambia la frase de ciruela oscuro...por estilo gentleman y si es posible añade el bigote de gentle que hay en gentle-shell" (L28). The Gentle theme's hint in Appearance reads "Estilo Gentleman" ("Gentleman style" in English); with the Gentleman mustache beside the theme name (L29).
- S7: Everything else keeps working as it does today (comments, pins, checks, Implement, Implement only this, Try another direction, compare, export, language, pan and zoom).
