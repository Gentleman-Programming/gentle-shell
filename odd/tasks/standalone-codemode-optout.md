# Standalone codemode opt-out (gentle-shell #1611 follow-up)

Silence Pi's `builtin:codemode` startup warning for user-owned Pi homes (`pi` + gentle-pi package, `gentle-shell --link`) while always keeping gentle-pi's compact codemode renderer, by asking the user once before writing `-builtin:codemode` to their settings.
Branch: `fix/1611-standalone-codemode-optout`. Delivery: `ask-on-risk`. Forecast: ~250 authored lines.

## Specs

- S1 — Keep the compact renderer. User: "Nosotros tenemos ese compacto porque cambiamos la forma en que mostramos la información, porque CodeMode es bastante verborrágico. Entonces, la idea sería cargar el nuestro, prácticamente, o cargar el que viene built-in y aplicarle nuestros cambios." gentle-pi keeps registering its compact `codemode` unconditionally whenever it does today; never fall back to the plain builtin renderer.
- S2 — Consent before writing (approved option "La 1"): "La primera vez que gentle-pi detecta que falta la exclusión, le pregunta al usuario si quiere agregar `-builtin:codemode` a `~/.pi/agent/settings.json`. Solo escribe si el usuario acepta. Así no tocamos su configuración sin permiso, y el warning desaparece desde el arranque siguiente."
- S3 — Scope of homes: "La opción 1 cubre las dos filas que hoy fallan, porque la pregunta va dentro de la extensión de gentle-pi y no en el launcher." Applies to `pi` + gentle-pi package and `gentle-shell --link`; isolated `gentle-shell` homes already carry the entry from #1612, so no prompt appears there.
- S4 — Safety of the write (inherited from #1612 launcher semantics): any explicit user entry for `builtin:codemode` (`-`, `+`, `!`, or bare) means no prompt and no write; malformed JSON, non-object settings, or non-array `extensions` means no write; other keys and entries, indentation, and trailing newline are preserved; a symlinked `settings.json` stays a symlink; no duplicate entries.
- S5 — Ask once: a declined prompt is remembered and not repeated on every launch. No prompt and no write without an interactive UI (print/RPC/non-interactive modes).
