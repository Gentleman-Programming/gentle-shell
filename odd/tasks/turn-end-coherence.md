## Specs

S1. One end-of-turn rule, stated positively and before any prohibition. Approved plan, step 2: "Escribir una sola regla de fin de turno, en positivo y al principio. Un turno termina solo si el resultado pedido está entregado y verificado, si espera trabajo ya lanzado, si falta una decisión o consentimiento tuyo, o si se agotó un límite explícito. Todo lo demás es un obstáculo: diagnosticar, cambiar de enfoque y seguir."
Examples the rule must decide the same way every time:
- A command, test or tool fails, times out or returns nothing, and another permitted approach exists: the turn continues (obstacle).
- A sub-step finished and the requested deliverable still needs a step the agent can run now (for example the asset is placed and the export was never launched): the turn continues and runs it.
- A background job or subagent the agent launched is still running and nothing independent remains: the turn ends (wait) and resumes on the completion event.
- A decision, consent or permission only the human can give is missing, or the human said stop: the turn ends for the work that applies to.
- An explicit one-run, no-retry or diagnostic-only limit, or an exhausted correction budget, covers the failed action: that action is not repeated; independent work continues.

S2. Approved plan, step 3: "Fijar tres términos y usarlos igual en todos lados. *Obstáculo* (fallo ordinario, se sigue), *límite* (explícito, vale solo para lo que nombra) y *espera* (trabajo lanzado, se retoma solo)." In English artifacts: obstacle, limit, wait. Text touched by this work never uses "stop", "blocked" or "denial" for an ordinary failure.

S3. Approved plan, step 4: "Acotar cada prohibición a su caso. Las reglas de workers, mantenimiento destructivo y presupuesto de corrección pasan a los módulos que se cargan cuando ese caso existe, o llevan su alcance al inicio de la frase." No existing protection is weakened: explicit human limits, provider consent, exact-token authorization, publishing and destructive-action permission, native review authority, worker prohibitions and the bounded recovery of #2919 keep their meaning.

S4. Approved plan, step 5: "Alinear herramientas y avisos con la regla. Reescribir la frase de timeout de `bash_background`, el resultado de lanzar un job y el aviso de fin de job para que digan lo mismo que el prompt." Current strings: "Report a timeout as failure; never automatically retry, restart, or migrate it to background." and "You will be notified once when it exits; keep working or end your turn." A timeout stays a reported failure and is never re-run unchanged; the text must say what to do next (read the output, fix the cause, then run again). The launch result and the job-exit notice must state continuation the way the subagent wake does ("Review it and continue.").

S5. Approved plan, step 7: "Dar paridad a GPT. Incluir en el prompt propio el equivalente de «Finishing work»." Reference text only Claude receives today, from its bridge: "Ending your turn means your work stops there until asked to continue, and you should not stop unless needed. Please avoid stopping while work the user asked for is still owed. [...] You may meet ordinary obstacles like errors, timeouts, locked files, empty results or failing tools. Diagnose them first, and when they are not real blockers, work through them with the access you have (wait and retry, fix the request, use another tool or source) rather than stopping or checking in. If someone clearly decided something is a hard blocker, such as a file marked not to be touched, access that was intentionally withheld, or a safety guardrail, leave it alone, say plainly what you found, and look for another way to finish the task." S1 may carry this meaning; it need not be a separate block.

S6. Constraints. Existing byte budgets hold (`tests/orchestrator-prompt-budget.test.ts`, `tests/orchestrator-budget.test.ts`): the change is byte-neutral or smaller; a budget is never raised here, an unavoidable overage is reported with its measured size. Tests that pin literal prompt, tool or notice text are updated with the text, never deleted to make room. Generated artifacts stay in English.
